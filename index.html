About Me

Yuhan is a Ph.D. candidate in Mechanical Engineering at Boston University. Her research combines electromagnetic simulation, mechanical design, RF prototyping, experimental testing, and quantitative validation to develop hardware systems for real-world sensing and imaging applications. Before this journey, She worked on wearable haptic interfaces at Tsinghua University, focusing on soft actuators, programmable tactile feedback, and human-centered interaction.

Yuhan enjoy exploring new ideas, working across disciplines, and collaborating with people from different backgrounds to build and test physical systems that connect engineering design with practical needs.
