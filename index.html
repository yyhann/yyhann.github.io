About Me

Yuhan is a Ph.D. student in Mechanical Engineering at Boston University. She develops wireless metamaterials that make MRI faster, clearer, more accessible, and more capable 🧲. Her work combines electromagnetic simulation, RF hardware design, prototyping, and experimental validation to translate physical concepts into practical medical imaging technologies. She also enjoys using coding to streamline research workflows, automate analysis, and improve productivity 💻.

Before joining Boston University, she worked on wearable haptic interfaces at Tsinghua University, focusing on soft actuators, programmable tactile feedback, and human-centered interaction. She is passionate about interdisciplinary research, practical engineering innovation, and collaborating with people from diverse technical and cultural backgrounds 🌍.

DESIGN AUTOMATION

CST Automation for Parametric RF Design

During my metamaterial-enabled MRI work, one resonator showed strong localized H-field enhancement but had a geometry too complex for conventional parameterization. I wrote CST scripts to generate the structure programmatically and automate design sweeps across key geometric variables. This made it possible to systematically trace performance trends across the design space and reduced simulation time for the project by roughly 50%.

DATA ANALYSIS AUTOMATION

Automated MRI SNR Analysis Tool

Comparing MRI validation datasets is a routine part of my work, but the original MATLAB workflow required manual code adjustments and offered limited visual interaction with analysis regions. I built an interactive web app that accepts two DICOM datasets, identifies signal and noise images, allows visual adjustment of the noise region, and generates side-by-side SNR maps with quantitative comparisons. It reproduces the established MATLAB calculation while making repeated scan analysis faster and more intuitive.

📄 Selected Publications

Circularly Polarized Metamaterial Cage for Homogeneous Signal-to-Noise Ratio Enhancement in Magnetic Resonance Imaging

Yuhan Liu, Xia Zhu, Ke Wu, Stephan W. Anderson, Xin Zhang

Advanced Materials, e16569

A wireless metamaterial cage constructed from coaxial cables is engineered for homogeneous SNR enhancement at 3.0 T. With its cylindrical geometry and electromagnetic architecture, the device supports circularly polarized resonance through engineered phase-shifted currents, enabling selective and omnidirectional interaction with the rotating B1- field to achieve a uniform magnetic field distribution. Integrated with the Birdcage coil (BC), the device yields a 31.45-fold SNR enhancement while maintaining comparable homogeneity to the BC alone, exhibiting only 12.07% variation within the region of interest (ROI).

Emotional and Directional Enabled Programmable Flexible Haptic Interface for Enhanced Cognition in Disabled Community

Yuhan Liu, Liuyang Han, Siqi Lv, Tao Jiang, Mingkai Duan, Hanyu Guo, Yuzhen Li, Qisen Xie, Yanru Chen, Dongkai Wang, et al.

Research, 8, 0714

A flexible, integrable, and programmable haptic interface based on elastomer actuators that utilize a unique forming process to create customized local stiffness in a multilayer elastomer by varying the cross-linking density of elastomers. Complemented by tailored software, the system delivers a 4-dimensional haptic experience within a safe voltage of less than 50 V and a frequency range of 50 to 450 Hz, enabling high-fidelity emotional and navigational feedback.
