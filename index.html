About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in Mechanical Engineering at Boston University, working at the intersection of biomedical imaging, electromagnetic metamaterials, and research-driven engineering.

My current research focuses on metamaterial-enabled MRI, especially wireless and patient-adaptive metamaterial interfaces for improving signal-to-noise ratio, spatial uniformity, and imaging flexibility in clinical MRI.
