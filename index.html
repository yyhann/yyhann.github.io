About Me

Yuhan Liu is a Ph.D. student in Mechanical Engineering at Boston University. She develops wireless metamaterials that make MRI faster, clearer, more accessible, and more capable. Her work combines electromagnetic simulation, RF hardware design, prototyping, and experimental validation to translate physical concepts into practical medical imaging technologies. She also enjoys using coding to streamline research workflows, automate analysis, and improve productivity.

Before joining Boston University, she worked on wearable haptic interfaces at Tsinghua University, focusing on soft actuators, programmable tactile feedback, and human-centered interaction. She is passionate about interdisciplinary research, practical engineering innovation, and collaborating with people from diverse technical and cultural backgrounds.
